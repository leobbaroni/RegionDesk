# RegionDesk

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected Electron + React + TypeScript. Windows desktop app with an embedded isolated browser. Code-first design, explicitly selected by the user.

## Users

A creator operating a new TikTok account from a Windows desktop, targeting US audiences or another selected region.

## Product Purpose

Configure and inspect a separate regional browsing environment. Keep account sessions, proxy routing and regional preferences together. RegionDesk is the working product name.

## Operating Context

The user chooses and supplies a proxy. Support free and paid providers with a total connection budget of $30/month. No required subscription for the local app. Browsing, login and publishing occur in the embedded browser. No video studio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Persistent isolated profiles; authenticated proxy connections; country, locale, timezone and browser location settings; permission controls; diagnostics; manual TikTok web publishing. Prefer a consistent saved environment. No artificial authentication cookies, fake server-side account history, or claims of genuine SIM service. Browser settings do not guarantee audience distribution. Live account and proxy validation requires user-provided access; never mark those checks passed without evidence. Fail closed for managed browsing when a configured proxy is unavailable. Do not change the host OS timezone or global proxy settings.

## Brand Commitments

Linear/Vercel-style dark SaaS interface. Restrained, compact, clear hierarchy. Code-first implementation.

## Evidence on Hand

No TikTok account credentials or proxy endpoint supplied. No existing app, brand assets or production metrics. Proxy comparison based on vendor documentation checked September 16, 2026; advertised prices require checkout verification.

## Product Principles

- Distinguish configured settings from measured results.
- Keep genuine sessions stable and isolated.
- Make the connection and active profile visible during browsing.
- Store credentials locally with OS-backed encryption when available.
- Keep the first version useful without a paid app service.
